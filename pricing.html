Good to know

Before you book.

Are studio fees included?

Studio rental is quoted separately when an indoor location is required. You will receive the full total before confirming your booking.

How do I choose the right collection?

Tell us the session type, preferred date, location and the photographs you hope to create. We will recommend the most suitable option.

How will I receive my photographs?

Your professionally edited images are delivered digitally in a convenient private gallery. Framed portraits are included only where stated.
